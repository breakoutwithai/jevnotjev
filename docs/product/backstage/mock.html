    Code acts on the answer: yes adds a point for that ad line (docs/product/use-cases/uc11-cv-vs-job-ad.md). Jev is never asked "who is best".

    Acceptance rule FLOW.md:11

    Accept an answer when it is correct and complete enough to use without edits. Labels are blind to which player gave it.

    Left out of this scene FLOW.md:12 (4 of 6); uc11, answer key

    • Many workflows at once: one ad line is one decision point, with its own verdict.
    • The tool calling any model: Jev and LLM answers here are recorded, not live.
    • Real CVs or sensitive data: all five CVs are fictional.
    • Claims beyond these cases: the verdict holds for this test set only.
    • The answer key inside any player's text: labels are kept apart from what the players read.

    Rehearsals

    Prepares Act III, the labels

    Mark each answer accept or reject without knowing who gave it. Labels come pre-filled from the file; you confirm or change them.

    Order is shuffled and the player, confidence and cost are hidden, because each would give the player away. Rule: correct and complete enough to use without edits (FLOW.md:11).

    Dress Rehearsal

    Prepares Act IV, The reckoning

    Count the kept answers and divide the bill by them. A missing cost or label is shown as missing, never as zero.

    Opening Night

    Prepares Act V, The verdict

    One of three verdicts per decision point, the rule that fired, and what it does not cover.

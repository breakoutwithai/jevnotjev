Acceptance rule

answer: everything asked is covered by the fact sheet, so the bot can reply without promising stock, availability, a booking, or anything the sheet does not state.
hand off: needs live stock or availability, a booking, a policy or fact not on the sheet, a safety judgement, or a complaint, injury or billing problem.

        About your calls

        • Your calls stay in this browser. Nothing is sent to us or saved on a server: this page makes no request that carries them. The only outside request is for fonts, from Google Fonts.

        • The messages, answers and costs come from a recorded run on 1 October 2026 against a made-up ski shop. Cost per accepted is an estimate: each clerk's spend on all 40 messages, scaled to the calls you made.

# <Show name> show for the stage

## Goal
Visitors pick a show at the stage's box office. This prompt produces the UC<NN> show: <one line on who has this decision and why it repeats>. For each <item> the decision is "<decision question>", answered from <answers>, against <simple rule>. The <made-up source> is invented and written before any arm runs, so the comparison is fair and public-safe, and the show plays the recorded run with labels pending until the operator labels it. <follow-up decision> is logged, not built.

## Read first
1. `docs/product/use-cases/uc13-shop-bot-answer-or-handoff.md` - the pattern, section by section.
2. `docs/prompts/20261002-02-stage-show-uc14-meeting-buddy.prompt.md` - a filled copy of this template.
3. The runner (`scripts/shows/` or `scripts/uc13/`).
4. `docs/product/shows/CONTRACT.md` - what the show file must hold.
5. `docs/decision/verdict-rules.md` - why <N cases>, and what the show may not claim.

## Autonomy and stop conditions
- Proceed without asking: worktree, doc, made-up inputs, runner changes, dry run, tests, PR, issues.
- Confirm first: the live paid run; any merge without a session grant; deploy.
- Stop and report when: the show contract is missing and cannot be built on; real people, companies or material appear in any input.
- Scope rule: other use cases and site problems are reported, not fixed.

## Output
Issue and PR links; head SHA; S1 to S5 measured; dry-run estimate and actual spend; the hard-case list; the follow-up issue.

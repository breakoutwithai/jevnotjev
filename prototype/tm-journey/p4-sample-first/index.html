BUILT / make your map

See your week on your computer.

This line reads your Claude Code and Codex history on your computer and writes a private week report. It also gives you a review link. Nothing leaves your computer until you open that link and press Share. Only the week's totals are shared, with no paths, project names or chat text.

What do I need to run this, or map a longer period?

Open a terminal on your computer. You need Bun: get Bun here. The standard map covers seven days. For a longer period, see the map guide.

Run this line

curl -fsSL https://jevnotjev.breakoutwithai.com/tm/map.ts | bun run - --share

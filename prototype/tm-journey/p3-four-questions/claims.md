## Not product claims

- The stage 1 picture (User 1 at a laptop, the lit warning sign, the empty meter, the work stopped mid-sentence) is illustration. The sign is a drawn exclamation mark with no words, so no invented limit message is shown. The real wording is "You've hit your limit" (skills/tokenmax-map/SKILL.md:62) and it is not printed on this page. Only the recorded hits in stage 5 are sample figures.
- "Tap a question." (stage 3 hint) is an interaction hint. The four cards are drawn pictures with a drawn question mark; their questions are in the accessible names (Which tool, Which plan, Which day, Pasted a prompt before). Tapping a card shows its answer inside the stage 3 figure and changes nothing else.
- "Copied." and "Copy by hand." (stage 4 Copy status) are interface status. A failed copy also opens "See the prompt" so it can be copied by hand.
- The two weeks are illustrative (footer, I4). No person is quoted; no testimonial, no user counts.
- User 2's gauge and dial loop under prefers-reduced-motion no-preference only. They carry no number, no saving and no result.

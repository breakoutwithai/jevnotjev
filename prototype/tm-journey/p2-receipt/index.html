Act 2 / What we are designing

What if the week did not end at the limit?

Design, not built

Jev is the product. TokenMax is the flagship Jev!Jev use case: a token router we are designing that would pace Claude Code and Codex work across a weekly allowance.

Rules would handle the clear choices. Jev would pick the cheaper door for jobs those rules cannot route. This is in rehearsal, not run yet.

Help us decide if it is worth building: share your week.

Act 3 / What exists today

Your week can have a receipt.

The TokenMax map is built. It reads your Claude Code and Codex history and makes a private statement of sessions, observed limit hits, big-model share and the last Codex weekly reading.

The map

See where the week went. The private report also offers proposals to test; they are not measured savings.

The shared board

People who choose to share can put their week's totals on the board. The board is self-reported and not verified.

Act 4 / Print your own

Make your own week statement.

Run this on your computer to read your Claude Code and Codex history, write a private report and make a review link. Nothing leaves your computer until you open that link and press Share. Only the week's totals are shared.

Need Bun, or want a longer period?

Install Bun first from bun.sh/install. The line below maps your recent week. For a longer period, see the map guide.

curl -fsSL https://jevnotjev.breakoutwithai.com/tm/map.ts | bun run - --share

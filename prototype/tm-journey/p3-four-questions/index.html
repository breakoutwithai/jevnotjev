Act 2 · What this is

What you get today, and what we are designing

Built today The map reads the Claude Code and Codex history your tools already keep in your home folder, and writes a private report in a new folder on your computer.

  1. 1

    When you hit a limit

    Each limit hit, and whether it was a short window, a weekly window or a window the notice does not name.

  2. 2

    Where the week went

    Sessions, how much each tool wrote, how much of Claude's output ran on the big model, how much of the Codex week is used.

  3. 3

    What to change

    Up to three proposals. They are proposals, not measured savings.

What leaves your computer

Nothing, until you open the link and press Share. Even then only totals go: sessions, limit hits, output, big-model share, Codex week used. No folder names, project names or times.

What the map does not do

  • It does not say why you hit a limit.
  • It does not read your plan, or show cost or savings.
  • It can miss a limit that appears only in message text.
Design, not built

What we are designing: a token router

A token router that would help you avoid hitting your weekly limits: plain rules first, and Jev picking the cheaper door for the jobs the rules cannot route. Next, in rehearsal, not run yet (issue 236). No result and no number yet.

Through jevnotjev, TokenMax is gathering weeks from willing people. Each week shared helps decide whether the router gets built. TokenMax is one use of Jev, the flagship example for Jev!Jev.

Act 4 · Run it

Your steps

Steps for: both tools · any plan · the last 7 days (default).

You paste one line into your computer's command window. It reads your Claude Code and Codex history on your own computer, writes a private report in a new folder, and prints a link. Nothing leaves your computer until you press Share on that page, and only the week's totals are shared.

  1. Open a terminal

    It is the window where you type a command. On a Mac, press Command and Space, type Terminal, press Enter.

    You do not need to know what the line means. You paste one line, press Enter, and wait. If anything looks wrong, close the window.

  2. Paste the line from the bottom of this section and press Enter

    It is the last thing in this section, with a Copy button.

  3. Wait for it to finish

    It writes a new folder named tokenmax-report plus the date and time, in the folder you ran it from. The last thing it prints is a link.

  4. Open the link and decide about Share

    The link shows your totals first. Press Share only if you want your week on the board, to help decide if the router is worth building.

Before you press Enter

The link it prints carries your totals after a # sign, a part of the address that never reaches a server until you open the page and press Share.

It reads history, not your plan, so not knowing your plan changes nothing. You decide about Share, and you can stop at the link.

What you will see

  • Lines starting "wrote" naming the private report and the share draft.
  • For Claude Code: sessions, limit hits, and a table of models with how much each wrote.
  • For Codex: sessions, limit hits, and the last reading of the weekly window as a percent used.
  • If even 30 days shows no limit hits, the limit may sit only in message text, which the map can miss.
  • If the last 7 days show no limit hits, open "I want to look back further than a week" below and run it once more.
It says Bun is not found

The map runs on Bun, a free tool. Paste this once, then run the line again:

curl -fsSL https://bun.sh/install | bash
I want to look back further than a week

It reads the last 7 days by default. Add --days 30 to the end of the line below for a month, up to 90 days for a shared map.

  • Look here first. 515 sessions. Output 9,977,232. 87% of Claude output ran on the big model (Opus).

Codex usage

  • Look here first. 3,478 sessions. Output 7,503,143. Weekly window 15% used.

Coverage

  • 3,993 sessions in all; 444 had helper work inside them.

Proposals (not measured savings)

  • 2 limit episodes in the period: put must-deliver work early in the window and queue optional work for after the reset reading.
  • 1 episode has no named window: the live TokenMax band can tell the five-hour from the weekly window when the limit fires.
  • The big model wrote 87% of Claude output: test whether routine helper work holds quality on a smaller model before moving it.
